QUARANTINE PROCEDURE - RETURNED BIOLOGICAL SPECIMENS

Revised following the events of 23 November. The revisions are marked.

PERIOD

Quarantine is ██ days. REVISED: quarantine is indefinite, and
release requires a finding, not the passage of time.

The former figure was arrived at by taking the longest incubation known
for the terrestrial diseases of the species and adding a margin. It
assumed the relevant clock was a disease clock. There is no basis for
that assumption and there never was.

ADMISSION

Specimen is examined, weighed, measured and photographed on admission.

REVISED: the specimen is weighed twice and BOTH figures are entered. The
standing instruction to discard the second figure is withdrawn.

That instruction existed because a scale that gives two answers is
usually a scale that needs calibrating. On 16 November the scale had been
calibrated that morning, the two figures differed, the second was
discarded as required, and the discrepancy went into a log as a
housekeeping note.

We have no idea whether it mattered. We have no idea because we threw the
number away.

DAILY OBSERVATION

Condition, appetite, respiration, temperature, cranial circumference.

REVISED: the observer records what the specimen does, not what the
specimen appears to feel. An entry stating that the subject is calm, or
content, or bored, is an interpretation of an animal by a person who
wants it to be an animal.

REVISED: the observer records what the SPECIMEN observes. Where it is
looking. What it turns towards. What it has stopped needing to be shown.

RELEASE

REVISED: no specimen is released from quarantine on the basis of good
health.

Good health was the presentation. For seven days this section examined a
healthy animal, wrote down that it was healthy, and was correct every
time. Every individual observation in that file is accurate. The file as
a whole is the most misleading document this station has produced.

A specimen that is well is not thereby empty.

STANDING NOTE

Procedure cannot be written against a thing that reads the procedure.

This section has revised what it can. What it cannot revise is that
everything in this document is available to anything that has been
watching a technician carry it.
