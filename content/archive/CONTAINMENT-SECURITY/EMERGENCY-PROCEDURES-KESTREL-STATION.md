EMERGENCY PROCEDURES - RECOVERY STATION KESTREL
REVISED EDITION - SUPERSEDES THE EDITION OF █████████████

ALARM STATES

    GREEN       Normal working. Bays occupied, protocols standing.
    AMBER       Anomaly reported in an occupied bay. Corridor cleared.
                Bay not entered. Photographic coverage retained.
    RED         Enclosure integrity in question. Level evacuated to
                ███████████. Entry by two, in equipment, on the
                Director's order only.
    BLACK       █████████████████████████████████████

    The BLACK state was added to this edition. The procedure for it is
    held at █████████████████ and is not distributed to the station. Staff
    are advised only that the alarm will sound as a continuous tone and
    that the instruction on hearing it is to remain where you are.

ON THE ALARM

Stop. Do not return for personal effects. Do not return for another
person unless you are directed to by the officer on the level.

Do not run in the corridors. A person running is a person who cannot be
counted, and the count is the whole of the procedure.

ASSEMBLY

Assemble by level, not by section. Give your number to the marshal.
Remain until you are released by name.

MEDICAL

Report every injury. There is no threshold. A scratch acquired at home on
a Sunday is reportable on Monday.

Personnel who report no injury are logged as having reported no injury.
That is the entirety of what the log means and it should not be read as
anything else.

FIRE

Unchanged from the previous edition. See sections 4 through 9.

NOTE ON THIS REVISION

The previous edition of these procedures ran to nine sections, of which
seven concerned fire, one concerned pressure, and one concerned the loss
of a specimen from a bay.

That last section assumed that a specimen leaving a bay would be an
animal, that it would be visible, that it would be somewhere, and that
the station's task would be to find it and put it back.

Every one of those assumptions was reasonable on █████████████ and not
one of them survived the twenty-third of November.

This edition does not replace them with better assumptions. It replaces
them with a count of people and a rule about hands, because those are the
two things this station can actually do.
