ANALYSIS OF THE DISPERSAL MECHANISM

Prepared from the five objects recovered from the walls of Bay 3 on
23 November and from necropsy findings.

THE OBJECTS

Five were recovered. All five are of the same construction and all five
came out of the subject.

Each is a shaped length of bone, approximately ███████ centimeters,
tapered, and barbed along the trailing portion. The barbs are cut so that
the object enters cleanly and does not return. Material has been
identified as host femur.

They were not grown. They were made. The cuts are consistent, they are
consistent with each other, and they are consistent with a maker working
to a design rather than to a shape.

MECHANISM OF EXPULSION

Necropsy establishes that each object was seated behind a major vessel
and expelled through the chest wall by the deliberate rupture of that
vessel. The pressure available by this method is more than sufficient to
cross a four meter room and to bury the object in a steel-lined wall,
which is where five of them were found.

The organism spent the last of the host on the throw.

ON THE DESIGN

I record the following because it is the finding of this report and not a
remark.

The honeybee leaves its sting in the wound. The bee does not survive it.
This is a well-known fact about a common insect and it is the only
mechanism in terrestrial biology that this device resembles.

The organism arrived here in November. It was in a sealed room with a
gray wall, a steel door and one window onto a corridor, and it had six
days.

It did not invent this in the enclosure. It learned it, and there is only
one place on this station it could have learned it from, and that is the
same place it learned the shift rotation, the layout of the corridors,
and the function of the lock on its own door.

It learned it from us.

TRANSMISSION

Contact is sufficient. The objects are a second route and a faster one.

Six were expelled. Five were recovered from the walls.

CONCLUSION

The enclosure was designed to prevent an animal from leaving a room. It
did that. It was never designed to prevent a room from putting something
into a corridor, and the object that is unaccounted for did not escape.

It was aimed.
