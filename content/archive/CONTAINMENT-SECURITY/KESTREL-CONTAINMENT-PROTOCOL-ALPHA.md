CONTAINMENT PROTOCOL ALPHA

EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY AND UNTIL WITHDRAWN IN WRITING

This station is now operating under Containment Protocol ALPHA. The
following supersedes all standing instruction where the two conflict.

1.  MOVEMENT

    No person is to enter a quarantine bay alone. No exception is
    delegated to shift supervisors.

    Movement between Levels ██ and ██ requires a
    countersigned pass. Passes are numbered and are collected.

2.  HANDLING

    Gloves at all times in transfer, examination and feeding. Gloves are
    to be █████████████████ on removal and are not to leave the bay.

    No person is to place a hand inside an enclosure while any other
    person is out of sight of the port.

3.  OBSERVATION

    Continuous photographic coverage of all occupied bays.

    Staff at the port are rotated daily, and the rotation is not to
    follow a pattern that can be learned. █████████ will draw the
    order and will not publish it in advance.

4.  REPORTING

    Any injury, however slight, is reportable within the hour. This
    includes injuries sustained off station and injuries whose cause is
    known and ordinary.

    A person who reports no injury is not thereby cleared. Reporting is
    a duty, not a finding.

5.  DEPARTURE

    No person leaves this station at the end of a shift without passing
    ███████████████████.

    This measure was in force from 1655 on 23 November. It is recorded
    here that it did not reach the gate in time to apply to the day
    shift of 23 November, and that this is the reason for its restatement
    in this protocol.

6.  SPEECH

    Personnel are not to discuss the work in the corridors, in the mess,
    on the pad, or within hearing of any bay whether occupied or not.

    This instruction has been read by some staff as a security measure.
    It is not a security measure. Refer to CONTINGENCY FILM 7-A, step
    seven, and to the reason step seven exists.

BY ORDER

The Director. Countersigned ███████████████, OCAC.
