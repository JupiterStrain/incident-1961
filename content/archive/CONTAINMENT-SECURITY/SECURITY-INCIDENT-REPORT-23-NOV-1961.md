INCIDENT REPORT - BAY 3 - 23 NOVEMBER 1961

Filed the same day, by the Director, from the corridor.

SUMMARY

At approximately 1400 hours the subject held in Bay 3 exceeded the
physical tolerance of its own skull. The event was not survivable and was
not intended by anything present.

The enclosure was sealed at the time and remained sealed throughout. It
is sealed now.

SEQUENCE

    0700    Feed accepted. Subject calm.
    1300    Feed accepted. Subject calm. Photographic series normal.
    1400    Terminal event. No personnel in Bay 3.
    1402    Corridor alarm. Corridor cleared to ███████████.
    ████    Entry by two, in protective equipment.
    1640    Recovery of material from the walls concluded.

CONDITION OF THE ENCLOSURE

The room is intact. There is no damage to the door, the frame, the port,
the hatch or the liner. The environmental systems ran without
interruption and are running now.

Five objects were recovered from the walls. They are described in
CONTAINMENT-FAILURE-ANALYSIS-REPORT and are not described here.

Six were expelled.

The sixth has not been recovered from Bay 3, and Bay 3 has now been
searched three times by three separate teams. It is not in the room.

WHAT THIS MEANS, STATED WITHOUT SOFTENING

The door was shut. The seals held. Every figure in the engineering
specification was met. On any ordinary reading of the word, containment
did not fail.

And there is an object unaccounted for, and it left a sealed room while
the room was sealed, and the only route out of Bay 3 that was open at
1400 was the observation port, which is laminated glass, and the
inspection hatch, which is █████████████████████.

I am not able to write the sentence that reconciles those two paragraphs.
I am recording both and sending this up tonight.

PERSONNEL

No personnel were in Bay 3 at 1400. No injury has been reported by any
member of station staff.

I want the phrasing of that second sentence noticed by whoever reads this.
It is what was reported. It is not the same as a finding, and I have
ordered the corridor rosters for the whole of today pulled and held.

ACTION TAKEN

Bay 3 sealed and posted. Station at █████████████. No departures
authorised pending █████████████████.

The order restricting departures was issued at 1655 and did not reach the
gate before the day shift had gone home.
