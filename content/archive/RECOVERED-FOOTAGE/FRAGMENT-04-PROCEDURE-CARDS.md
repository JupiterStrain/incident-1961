TRANSFER NOTE - FRAGMENT 04

The last three of the seven procedure cards, and the card that follows them.

The demonstration footage cut against these cards becomes less useful as the
cards proceed. By the sixth card the demonstration is a closed door. Film
Section assumes this is a fault of assembly at the laboratory and notes that
the same fault appears on both prints held here.

The narrator's delivery does not change across these cards. The warmth of
the opening reel is retained through card seven, which is the point at which
the instruction stops describing how to handle an animal.

The card held after step seven reads:

    YOU ARE PERMITTED TO BE AFRAID.
    YOU ARE NOT PERMITTED TO BE INTERESTING.

It is held for eight seconds on black. Film Section has timed it three times
because the duration was queried. Eight seconds is correct.
