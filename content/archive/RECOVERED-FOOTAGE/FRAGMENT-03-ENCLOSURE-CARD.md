TRANSFER NOTE - FRAGMENT 03

A title card, followed by a photographic plate.

The card is legible and is transcribed here in full for the paper record:

    THE ENCLOSURE WAS SEALED.
    THE ENCLOSURE WAS NOT THE CONTAINMENT.

The plate that follows is an infrared astronomical exposure and does not
belong to the instructional material either side of it. There is no splice.
It was photographed onto this print as part of the film.

Film Section has been asked whether the plate matches the one held in
TELEMETRY-RESEARCH. It does. We are not able to say how a plate exposed at
█████████████ came to be in a training film about the recovery of animals.

The annotation in the corner of the plate is legible on the negative and
is not legible on this transfer.
