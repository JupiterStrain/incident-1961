EVALUATION OF STAFF - BIOLOGICAL SECTION
CONDUCTED 8-11 DECEMBER 1961

Six members of the Biological Section were interviewed following the
events of 23 November. Interviews were voluntary and none were declined.

GENERAL

Section is functioning. Attendance normal. Work of the required standard.
No member has requested transfer.

INDIVIDUAL NOTES, ANONYMISED

    ████  Sleeping poorly. Attributes it to the overtime.
              Fit for duty.

    ████  Declines to work Level ██ alone. This has been
              accommodated and costs the section nothing.
              Fit for duty.

    ████  The technician who declined the measurement on
              22 November. Composed. Asked whether the decision had
              gone against him and was told it had not.

              Asked, unprompted, whether the subject had understood
              that he was refusing. Interviewer had no answer. Subject
              did not raise it again.
              Fit for duty.

    ████  No adverse findings.

    ████  No adverse findings. Requested that the observation
              logs be removed from the section office. Request granted.

    ████  Section head. See below.

SECTION HEAD

Working excessive hours. Has produced the analysis of 28 November, the
revised quarantine procedure and two further papers in nineteen days.

Interviewed at length. Composed throughout. Asked to state any concern
for the record and stated one, which is reproduced here in full at his
request:

    "Every observation my section made was accurate. I have checked
    them all. There is not a wrong figure in that file. We measured
    what we were asked to measure, at the hour we were asked to
    measure it, and we wrote down what we saw, and what we saw was a
    healthy animal, and it was a healthy animal.

    I would like it on the record that the procedure worked and that
    it did not help. Somebody is going to read that file in twenty
    years and look for the mistake, and they are going to keep
    looking, because it isn't in there."

Fit for duty. Recommend leave, which he has declined.

OVERALL

No member of this section requires removal from post.

The evaluation notes, without recommendation, that the six people
interviewed asked a total of nine questions, of which seven concerned
what the subject had known and none concerned what it had done.
