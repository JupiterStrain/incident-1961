NOTE APPENDED BY THE DIRECTOR, 30 NOVEMBER 1961

The Biological Section report of 28 November states that in the course of
seven days the subject acquired the layout of the station, the shift
rotation, and the names of eleven staff.

Eleven.

Personnel Section has been asked to determine which eleven. It cannot,
and I do not believe the question can be answered by any method available
to this station.

I record the following instead, for whoever reads this file later.

Twelve members of this establishment had duty within sight of the
observation port between the sixteenth and the twenty-third of November.

Eleven of them wore a name tape.

The twelfth is Employee 4119, who is Plant and Services, whose overall
does not carry one, who had no business on that level, and whose name the
subject had no way to learn.
