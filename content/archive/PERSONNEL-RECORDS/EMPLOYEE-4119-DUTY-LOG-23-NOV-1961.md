DUTY LOG - PLANT AND SERVICES - 23 NOVEMBER 1961
EXTRACT: EMPLOYEE 4119

Entries in the employee's own hand. Reproduced as written, including the
last one.

    0600    On. Plant room. Pressure normal.
    0640    No 2 compressor, replaced █████████. Running sweet.
    0715    Dock. Two crates in, signed for.
    0830    Level ██ corridor lamp out, replaced.
    0905    Boiler. Blowdown.
    1010    Break.
    1035    Dock. Nothing.
    1120    Level ██, radiator ███ knocking. Bled it.
    1200    Lunch.
    1245    Plant room. Pressure normal.
    1330    Went down.
    1402    Alarm.
    1730    Off. Overtime 3.5 hrs, cleared with ███████████.

NOTE BY THE SUPERVISOR, 24 NOVEMBER

The entry at 1330 reads "Went down." That is the whole of it. There is no
level, no job, no plant item and no signature against it, and in
██ years this man has never written a line like that in a log.

Every other entry on this page is in the form he has used since he was
hired: place, item, action. He writes "No 2 compressor". He writes
"radiator knocking". He does not write "went".

I have asked him what the entry means. He read it, and he agreed it was
his handwriting, and he could not tell me what it referred to.

He was not distressed by this. That is the part I am putting in the file.
A man who cannot account for thirty minutes of his own day is usually
worried about it. He looked at the line the way you would look at
somebody else's.

I am not qualified to say what that indicates and I am not going to
guess. I am recording it because I was in the room and nobody else was.
