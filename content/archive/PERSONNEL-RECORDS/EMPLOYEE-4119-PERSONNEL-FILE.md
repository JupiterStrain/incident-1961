NOTE ADDED 24 NOVEMBER 1961

Employee 4119 was on the establishment on 23 November 1961 and worked a
full day shift, 0600 to 1400, extended to 1730 by the alarm.

He was not assigned to Bay 3. He was not assigned to Level ██.
He had no duty in the corridor outside Bay 3 at any point on that date,
and the maintenance schedule for the week does not place him there.

He was in that corridor at approximately 1400 hours.

He has been asked why, twice. He has given the same answer both times,
which is that he does not remember going down there and assumed he had
been called.

No call was logged.

He reported no injury. He completed his shift. He signed out at the gate
at 1741, ahead of the restriction, and drove home.
