SPLASHDOWN AREA - SURVEY AND CLEARANCE

Survey conducted 18-23 November of the recovery area and of the transit
corridor between splashdown and Recovery Station Kestrel.

PURPOSE

To establish whether material was shed into the environment between
splashdown and quarantine, and to determine whether clearance of the area
is required.

METHOD

Water sampling at ██ stations. Air sampling aboard the
recovery vessel and at pad ███. Swab sampling of the
transport container, the rotorcraft cabin, and the corridor between the
pad and Bay 3.

FINDINGS

    Water, all stations           Negative
    Air, recovery vessel          Negative
    Air, pad and apron            Negative
    Transport container           ██████████████
    Rotorcraft cabin              ██████████████
    Corridor, pad to Bay 3        ██████████████

QUALIFICATION, AND IT IS A SERIOUS ONE

Every negative in the table above was returned by a method that was
designed before this station knew what it was looking for.

The sampling protocol used here filters at █████████ and identifies
by █████████████████████. As of the analysis of 28 November we understand the
material of interest to be smaller than the first figure and invisible
to the second.

This survey therefore establishes that the recovery area is free of
anything this survey is capable of detecting. It does not establish that
the recovery area is clear, and it should not be filed as though it does.

I have asked that the water samples be retained rather than discarded at
ninety days. I have been told the retention order requires █████████████████.

RECOMMENDATION

No clearance action at this time. Resurvey when a method exists.

I would record one further observation. The area of ocean in question is
not a room and cannot be closed. If the finding of 28 November is correct
and the material was aboard the vehicle before splashdown, then the
question of what was shed into the water at 0812 is not answerable by
survey, and the honest entry in this file is that we do not know and have
no means of knowing.
