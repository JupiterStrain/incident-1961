CONSOLIDATED TIMELINE - PROJECT APEX - NOVEMBER 1961

Compiled from the deck log, the transfer log, the observation series and
the incident report. Times as recorded. Discrepancies noted.

16 NOVEMBER

    ████    Launch.
    0812    Splashdown.
    0903    Hatch opened. Occupant alive, calm, in good condition.
    0940    Quarantine established, Bay 3.
    1400    Cranial circumference 27.4 cm. Baseline.

17 NOVEMBER      D+1     Observation log 001. Condition good.
18 NOVEMBER      D+2     27.9 cm.
19 NOVEMBER      D+3     29.1 cm. Independent veterinary examination.
                         Reported a healthy animal.
                         First recorded anticipation of shift change.
20 NOVEMBER      D+4     30.8 cm. Observation log 002.
                         Subject waits at the hatch before the technician
                         is in view of the port.
21 NOVEMBER      D+5     32.6 cm.
22 NOVEMBER      D+6     MEASUREMENT DECLINED. Observation log 003.
                         Two staff decline to open the enclosure.
                         Formal request for review of station authority.

23 NOVEMBER

    0700    Feed accepted.
    1300    Feed accepted.
    1400    Terminal event. See SECURITY-INCIDENT-REPORT-23-NOV-1961.
    1402    Alarm. Corridor cleared.
    ████    Bay 3 entered by ███████████.
    1640    Five recovered from the walls.
    ████    Sixth not recovered. Search of the corridor begun.
    2200    Shift change. Station released to night rotation.

    Note: the request of 22 November for review of station authority was
    scheduled for consideration on 25 November.

24 NOVEMBER      Gloves mandatory in transfer. Enclosure not to be opened
                 by any individual working alone.
28 NOVEMBER      Biological analysis issued. Organism identified as
                 single-celled and colonial.
29 NOVEMBER      Recovery detachment interviewed a third time.
30 NOVEMBER      This timeline compiled.

DISCREPANCY

The observation series records six daily measurements, D+1 to D+6, taken
at 1400. The terminal event occurred at approximately 1400 on D+7.

Three separate accounts state that the 1400 measurement on 23 November
was not attempted, and give three different reasons. No measurement was
scheduled to be taken by anyone who was subsequently in the corridor.

Records Division notes without comment that the terminal event occurred
at the hour of the daily measurement, on the first day nobody came.
