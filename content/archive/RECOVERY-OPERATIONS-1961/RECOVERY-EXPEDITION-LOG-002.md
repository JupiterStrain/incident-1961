TRANSFER LOG - OCCUPANT AND VEHICLE

0928    Transport container loaded, rotorcraft █████████.
0931    Airborne.
0939    On deck, Recovery Station Kestrel, pad ███.
0940    Occupant received by Biological Section. Quarantine established.

Elapsed from hatch to quarantine: thirty-seven minutes. This is within
the standing figure of forty-five and is the fastest transfer this
detachment has recorded.

PERSONNEL IN CONTACT WITH THE OCCUPANT

Between hatch opening and quarantine, the occupant was handled by four
persons and was within arm's length of a further ██. All are
named in the station roster of that date.

Gloves were worn at the hatch. Gloves were not worn during the transfer
from container to enclosure, which was not a requirement at the time and
which became a requirement on 24 November.

VEHICLE DISPOSITION

Vehicle held aboard the recovery vessel overnight, offloaded 17 November
at ███████████, and forwarded to █████████████████ for teardown.

Teardown findings are filed separately. Summary as reported to this
detachment: no breach, no fault, no anomaly, and the cabin atmosphere
sampled on 17 November was █████████████████████.

REMARK

The vehicle was clean. Every inspection has said so. The occupant was
healthy. Every examination has said so.

The station has spent a fortnight asking how something got in. On the
evidence available to this detachment, nothing got in. We flew up through
it, and then we brought it down, and we did that on purpose, and we did it
correctly, and we have the paperwork to show we did it correctly.
