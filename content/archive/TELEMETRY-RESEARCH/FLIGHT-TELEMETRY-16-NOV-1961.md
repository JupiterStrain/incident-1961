BODY TEMPERATURE

Baseline at the pad. Rise commencing at T+█████, reaching four
degrees above baseline at approximately T+█████, and holding at
that figure for the remainder of the flight.

The rise is early. It is not consistent with the thermal profile of the
cabin, which was within limits throughout, and it is not consistent with
exertion, because the activity channel is flat across the same interval.

Telemetry Section is asked routinely to explain a four degree rise in a
returned animal and can usually do so. On this profile we cannot.
