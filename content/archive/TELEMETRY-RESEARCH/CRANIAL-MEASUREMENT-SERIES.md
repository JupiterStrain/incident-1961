ANALYSIS

Five figures is not a series and this section said so when it was asked.
It has been asked again, so the following is offered with that
qualification standing.

The increments are 0.5, 1.2, 1.7, 1.8. The differences between the
increments are 0.7, 0.5, 0.1.

That is not acceleration. That is a curve that is flattening.

If the four increments are fitted and the fit is projected, the series
approaches a limit in the region of ████ centimeters. The
projection puts the approach at D+7 or D+8.

We are asked what happens at the limit. We are a telemetry section and
the answer is outside our competence. What we can state is that the
series does not run away. It arrives somewhere.

The Biological Section report of 28 November describes the terminal event
as the physical tolerance of the skull being exceeded. Our figures are
consistent with that and add one thing to it.

The curve was slowing down. Whatever was doing this was finishing.

D+6 AND D+7

The entry for D+6 is MEASUREMENT DECLINED and is correctly recorded under
Station Protocol Manual section 6. It is not a gap.

There is no entry for D+7. The measurement was scheduled for 1400.
