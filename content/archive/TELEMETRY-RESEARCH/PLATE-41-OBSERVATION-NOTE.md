DESCRIPTION

Two curved traces, entering the field from opposite margins and
converging.

At the point of convergence, a return of ███████████. The observer
notes that the figure exceeds the calibrated range of the plate and that
the value given is a floor and not a measurement.

The traces are not stars. They are not a defect of the emulsion; a second
plate exposed on █████████████ shows the same geometry advanced by
█████████. They are not aircraft, not satellite, and not a known
█████████████████.

They are moving. Slowly, and towards each other, and they have been doing
it for as long as anyone has thought to look, which is ███████████.

ANNOTATION ON THE PLATE

In grease pencil, in the corner, in the observer's hand:

    PLATE 41 - 1961 - NOT JUPITER

TRANSMITTAL NOTE, OCAC

This plate is forwarded to the Kestrel file at the request of
███████████████ and over the objection of this Branch.

The objection is recorded as follows. There is no established connection
between the events at Recovery Station Kestrel in November and an
infrared return in a field █████████████████ away from anything this
Office is concerned with. Filing the two together creates a connection
that the evidence does not support, and once filed it will be read.

The request was granted on the ground that the observatory's annotation
denies a connection which nobody had proposed.

No further distribution. No copies. This note is not to be summarised in
the monthly return.
