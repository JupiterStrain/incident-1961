MORPHOLOGY

Single cell. Approximately ███████████. No █████████. Wall
█████████████████. Contents █████████████████████.

At this magnification the cell is unremarkable. Placed in a field beside
████████████████████████, a competent technician would not separate them, and
this section did not separate them between 16 and 23 November.

We have re-examined the slides prepared on admission. The material was on
them. It was on the first slide made in this building, prepared at 1105
on 16 November, and it was recorded on that occasion as debris.

BEHAVIOR IN CULTURE

Isolated cells: nothing. No motility, no division under any medium tried,
no response to ██████████████. A single cell is inert and is, so far as
this section can determine, not doing anything at all.

Cells above a density of approximately ███████████: division begins.

Above ███████████: coordination. The population moves together
towards █████████████████ and away from ███████████, and the movement
is not the sum of the individual movements, because there are no
individual movements below the density.

We do not have a term for this. This section has been using "the
population decides", which is not a scientific statement and which we
will stop using once someone offers us a better one.

ESTIMATE

Colony at terminal, by volume and by count on the necropsy sections, on
the order of █████████████ cells.

NOTE

The station has spent three weeks asking what got into the enclosure.

The answer is that on 16 November approximately █████████ cells came
down inside a healthy animal, and that number is small enough to sit on a
slide and be called debris, and it is small enough that if it had arrived
four hours later there would not have been enough of it to do anything at
all.

The flight was four hours and eleven minutes.
