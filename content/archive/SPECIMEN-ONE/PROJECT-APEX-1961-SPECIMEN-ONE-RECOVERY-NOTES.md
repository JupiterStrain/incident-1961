SPECIMEN RECOVERY - FIELD NOTES

VEHICLE:        ███████████████████
OCCUPANT:       Subject 7, chimpanzee, male, ██ years
FLIGHT:         4 hours 11 minutes
LAUNCH:         16 November 1961, ████████ hours
SPLASHDOWN:     16 November 1961, 0812 hours
RECOVERY AREA:  █████████████████████████████████

SEQUENCE

0812    Splashdown observed. Beacon acquired.
0819    Recovery vessel ██████████████ on station.
0827    Swimmers in water. Flotation collar fitted.
0851    Vehicle aboard.
0903    Hatch opened. Occupant alive, responsive, calm.
0917    Occupant transferred to transport container.
0940    Quarantine established, Recovery Station Kestrel, Bay 3.

CONDITION AT RECOVERY

Occupant was found alive and in good condition. It accepted a piece of
fruit from ████████████ within four minutes of the hatch being
opened, which is recorded here because it is the customary informal test
of a returned animal and because the animal passed it.

Respiration nominal. No injury. No sign of distress. Temperature elevated
four degrees.

VEHICLE CONDITION

Hull intact. No breach. No deformation. Heat shield within tolerance.
Recovery crew report the vehicle was ████████████████████████ on opening.

Seal integrity was checked at 0903 and again at 1120 and was found
satisfactory on both occasions. I state this because it has been asked
twice since, in both cases by people who were not present: nothing came
through the hull of this vehicle, because nothing had to.

The cabin was at ███████████████████ for the duration of the flight.

REMARKS

Flight duration is 4 hours 11 minutes. That figure is correct as flown and
is not a transcription error. Queries regarding the duration are to be
referred to ████████████████ and are not to be answered at station level.

Personnel present at hatch opening: six. Personnel in contact with the
occupant between 0903 and 0940: four. All four are named in
PERSONNEL-RECORDS and all four were, at the time of writing, well.
