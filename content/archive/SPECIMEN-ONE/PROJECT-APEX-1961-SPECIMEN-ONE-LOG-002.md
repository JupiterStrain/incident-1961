SUBJECT 7 - OBSERVATION LOG - D+4

CONDITION

Unchanged. This is stated plainly because it continues to be true and
because it continues to be the difficulty.

Subject is in good health by every measure available to this section.
Respiration nominal. Appetite normal. Coat condition good. Temperature
elevated four degrees and holding, as it has held since recovery, without
variation of more than ██████ of a degree in ninety-six hours.

There is no sign of infection. There is no sign of injury. There is no
sign of distress. A veterinarian brought in from ████████████████████ and
given no briefing examined the subject on 19 November and reported a
healthy animal.

CRANIAL CIRCUMFERENCE: 30.8 CM

    D+2     27.9
    D+3     29.1

Engineering has re-verified the tape and the technician has been observed
taking the measurement. The measurement is correct.

I record the following for the file. A chimpanzee of this age does not
gain three and a half centimetres of cranial circumference in three days.
A chimpanzee of this age does not gain three and a half centimetres of
cranial circumference. The subject is not in discomfort, is not favouring
its head, and shows no sign of the ████████████████████████ that would
accompany any process I am able to name.

BEHAVIOUR

Subject continues to observe hands.

On 19 November at approximately 1100, technician ████████ entered
the corridor outside the enclosure carrying a tray. Subject was seated
facing the port, as always. It stood, crossed the enclosure, and waited
at the point on the wall ███████████████████████████████ — which is where the hatch
opens — before the technician had come into view of the port.

The technician's route that morning was not the usual route.

I am told this is coincidence and I have no evidence to offer against
that. I am recording the time and the name so that the file is complete.

NOTE APPENDED BY █████████████

The animal has begun to anticipate. Log it, do not interpret it, and do
not put this in the daily summary that leaves the station.

RECOMMENDATION

Continue observation. Increase to continuous coverage. Personnel at the
port are to be rotated daily and are not to be rotated in a pattern.
