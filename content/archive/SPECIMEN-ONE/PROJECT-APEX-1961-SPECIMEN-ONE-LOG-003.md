SUBJECT 7 - OBSERVATION LOG - D+6

CONDITION

Subject is in good health.

I have written that sentence six times in six days and I would like the
record to show that I no longer know what it means.

Respiration nominal. Appetite normal. Temperature elevated four degrees
and holding. Subject accepted food at 0700 and at 1300. Subject accepted
handling. Subject has not once resisted, refused, startled, or shown any
sign of pain.

CRANIAL CIRCUMFERENCE: MEASUREMENT DECLINED

The series to date:

    D+1     27.4
    D+2     27.9
    D+3     29.1
    D+4     30.8
    D+5     32.6
    D+6     MEASUREMENT DECLINED

The measurement was not taken. Technician ██████████████ approached the
enclosure at 1400 as scheduled, in the presence of two staff, and did not
open the hatch. He was not prevented from opening it. When asked, he
stated that he did not want to put his hands in.

He has been on this station eleven years. I have entered no disciplinary
note and I do not intend to.

I attempted the measurement myself at 1520 and did not complete it
either. I am recording this in full because a gap in the series will
otherwise be read as an oversight. It was not an oversight. Two people
who are paid to put their hands in an enclosure declined to put their
hands in an enclosure, and neither of us can give a reason that would
survive being written down.

BEHAVIOUR

Subject is calm. Subject remains seated facing the observation port for
the majority of each day. It eats, it drinks, it sleeps on the ordinary
schedule for the species.

It knows the shift change. It has known the shift change since D+3. At
2200, before the corridor door opens, it turns to face the corridor.

At 1640 today subject was observed at the enclosure door with its hand
resting flat on the plate ████████████████████████████, which is not a place an
animal has any reason to touch. It was not trying the door. It was not
pulling at it. It had its hand on the part of the door that ███████████,
and it held it there for a little over two minutes, and then it went and
sat down again.

Dr. ████████████ has asked me to state a hypothesis. I decline. I will
state an observation instead: for six days we have been standing at the
port watching the subject, and I am no longer confident about the
direction of that sentence.

RECOMMENDATION

Enclosure is not to be opened by any individual working alone.

Measurement series to be suspended pending ████████████████████.

I am requesting, formally and in writing, that the station's authority to
█████████████████████████████████████ be reviewed before D+8.
