SPECIMEN ONE - BIOLOGICAL ANALYSIS

Prepared five days after the terminal event, from material recovered from
the enclosure, from the recovered darts, and from tissue taken at
necropsy from Subject 7.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The station has spent seven days observing the wrong thing.

Every observation log in this file describes Subject 7. Subject 7 was an
animal. Subject 7 was, throughout, exactly the healthy chimpanzee it
appeared to be, and the veterinarian who examined it on 19 November was
not mistaken.

Specimen One was never in the enclosure in the sense we have been using
that word. Specimen One was in Subject 7, and Specimen One is not
visible to the unaided eye at any magnification available on this
station below █████████.

CELLULAR FINDINGS

The organism is single-celled. Individual cells measure ███████████
and are, in isolation, unremarkable, inert, and readily mistaken for
████████████████████████ on a poorly prepared slide. We have almost certainly
been discarding them since the sixteenth.

Isolated cells do nothing. Cultured in quantity they do nothing that
resembles the behavior recorded in the observation logs. What was in
the enclosure was not a cell. It was a colony, and by our estimate at
terminal it numbered on the order of █████████████ individual cells
occupying the volume of the animal.

Whatever was watching our hands was the population, not the cell.

CONSEQUENCES, STATED PLAINLY BECAUSE THEY BEAR ON CONTAINMENT

1.  No breach was required at any point. A hull is proof against an
    organism. It is not proof against a cell, and the vehicle's seals
    were rated against pressure, not against █████████████████.

2.  Establishment is slow. Reproduction is not. A handful of cells can
    do nothing to an animal; they must divide for hours before there is
    a colony large enough to do anything at all. This is why the flight
    profile matters and why █████████████████████████████.

3.  There is no organism to contain. There is only an infected volume,
    and a wall is a boundary of the wrong kind.

4.  The cranial series was not growth in the sense of disease. The
    colony was ████████████████████ the host's brain in order to use it, and
    it continued past the point where the structure could bear it.

    We have been describing the outcome as a death. It is better
    understood as an ██████████████. The organism does not benefit
    from the death of a host and, on this evidence, does not intend it.
    It simply could not stop.

THREAT ASSESSMENT

The organism preserves the host. It keeps the host alive, in health, and
— we now believe — ███████████. It does not consume the host in any
ordinary sense. It takes what the host knows.

Transmission is by contact and by the mechanism documented in
CONTAINMENT-FAILURE-ANALYSIS.

We record the following without qualification: nothing in the seven days
of observation looked like an outbreak. It looked like a well-run
quarantine of a healthy animal. That is the finding of this report, and
it is the reason this section can no longer certify any procedure that
begins by asking a technician to observe a subject and report what is
wrong with it.

Nothing was wrong with it. That was the presentation.

One final observation, which this section offers without recommendation.

This work is conducted under a project whose authorised scope is the
examination of primates returned from flight. That scope was not exceeded
at any point. For seven days this station examined a primate returned from
flight, thoroughly, correctly, and to the limit of its instruments.

The authorising instrument did not contemplate that the primate might not
be the specimen. Neither did we.
