SUBJECT 7 - OBSERVATION LOG - D+1

Subject 7 was transferred to the enclosure at 0940 on 16 November and has
now completed one full observation day. This log covers 0600 to 1800.

CONDITION

Subject is in good health. Respiration nominal. Appetite normal. Subject
accepted food, accepted water, and accepted handling without distress of
any kind. Coat condition good. No lesion, no discharge, no tremor, no
guarding of any limb.

Core temperature is elevated four degrees above the species resting
baseline and has held at that figure without variation for twenty-eight
hours. It has neither risen nor fallen. Dr. ████████████ notes that a
fever which does not move is not behaving as a fever.

Body mass was recorded twice on admission, per procedure. The two figures
did not agree. Procedure requires that the second figure be discarded and
it has been discarded. The discrepancy is ███████████████████████ grams and
is noted here only because the scale was calibrated the same morning.

CRANIAL CIRCUMFERENCE: 27.4 CM

Recorded at 1400 by ██████████████. This is the baseline figure. All
subsequent measurements are to be taken by the same technician, at the
same hour, with the same tape.

BEHAVIOUR

Subject is calm. Subject is markedly calmer than the two control animals
housed in ██████████████████, both of which flew shorter profiles and both
of which are behaving as returned animals normally behave.

Subject spent the observation period seated upright and facing the
observation port. It did not pace. It did not attempt the door. It did
not respond to the presence of staff in the corridor except by looking
at them.

One item is recorded at the request of ███████████: subject watches
hands. Not faces. When a technician is at the port, subject's attention
is on the hands, and it follows them when they move.

I do not regard this as significant and I am recording it because I was
asked to record it.

RECOMMENDATION

Continue observation at present frequency. No change to environmental
parameters. No change to feeding. Cranial measurement daily at 1400.

Subject 7 is the calmest animal in the building.
