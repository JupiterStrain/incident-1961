ENCLOSURE SPECIFICATION - BAY 3 - BIOLOGICAL QUARANTINE

Issued on transfer of Subject 7, 16 November 1961.

DESIGNATION:    Bay 3, Biological Quarantine, Recovery Station Kestrel
STANDARD:       OCAC █████████ / Class II animal quarantine

STRUCTURE

    Interior            4.0 m x 4.0 m x 3.5 m
    Wall                60 cm reinforced concrete, steel liner
    Floor               ███████████████████████████████
    Ceiling             ███████████████████████████████
    Door                Single leaf, steel, ██████████████
    Observation port    Laminated glass, 40 cm x 40 cm, corridor side

ENVIRONMENTAL

    Temperature         18-24 C
    Atmosphere          Station air, single pass, exhausted to ███████████
    Humidity            45-55 percent
    Lighting            Continuous

    Note: air handling for Bay 3 is single-pass and is exhausted through
    the ██████████████ common to Bays 1 through 4. Filtration is rated
    for particulate at █████████ and above.

OBSERVATION

    Photographic        One fixed camera, corridor side, 3-second interval
    Biological          Thermocouple, respiration cuff
    Audio               None fitted
    Barrier integrity   Pressure differential, door frame

FEEDING PROTOCOL

    Frequency           Twice daily, 0700 and 1300
    Delivery            Hatch, corridor side, manual
    Handling            Two persons. One at hatch, one at port.

EMERGENCY

    In the event of breach, activate ██████████████████████ and evacuate to
    Level ██. Bay 3 cannot be sealed from within the corridor.
    This is a known deficiency of the 1959 fit-out and was raised in
    ██████████████████.

ENGINEERING NOTE, ADDED 24 NOVEMBER 1961

This specification was correct. Every figure in it was met and every
system in it performed to specification on 23 November.

The chamber held. The door held. The seals held. Nothing in the structure
failed, and I want that in the file, because the report being prepared
upstairs refers to a containment failure and Engineering did not have one.

We were asked to build a box that would keep an animal in a room. We
built it. Nobody asked us for a box that would keep ███████████████████████████████,
and if they had we would have told them that this station does not
have one and that the drawings for one do not exist.

The enclosure was sealed. The enclosure was not the containment.
