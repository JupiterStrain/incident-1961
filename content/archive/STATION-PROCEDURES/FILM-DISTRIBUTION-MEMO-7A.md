MEMORANDUM - DISTRIBUTION OF CONTINGENCY FILM 7-A

1.  CONTINGENCY FILM 7-A is released for instructional use at all
    recovery stations effective this date. Prints are being despatched
    from ███████████████ at the rate of ██ per week.

2.  THIS FILM SUPERSEDES CONTINGENCY FILM 7.

3.  CONTINGENCY FILM 7 SHOULD NOT BE VIEWED.

    Prints of Film 7 held at station level are to be returned to the
    Branch under seal. They are not to be run for the purpose of
    comparison. They are not to be run for the purpose of confirming
    that they are the correct reels.

    Two stations have asked how they are to identify a print they are
    not permitted to view. The answer is the can, which is stencilled.
    A station which cannot identify a print from the can should return
    every print it holds.

4.  THERE IS NO CONTINGENCY FILM 8.

    The Branch is aware that a Film 8 was announced in the schedule
    circulated in █████████████ and was listed as in preparation for
    some months afterwards.

    Enquiries regarding Film 8 are to be directed to the Branch and are
    not to be answered at station level.

5.  Film 7-A runs eight minutes and is complete as supplied. Where a
    print appears to run short, or where the projectionist reports a
    splice he did not make, the print is to be withdrawn and the Branch
    notified by ███████████.

    Do not repair the print. Do not run the repaired print to check the
    repair.

6.  The film is instructional and is not a record. Nothing in it is to be
    read as documenting a particular station, a particular flight or a
    particular specimen, and the Branch will not confirm the origin of
    any footage it contains.

    Station Directors receiving questions on this point from their staff
    should refer them to paragraph 6 of this memorandum, which is the
    whole of the Branch's position and is not going to be enlarged.
