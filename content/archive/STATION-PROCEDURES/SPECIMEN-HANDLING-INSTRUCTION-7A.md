HANDLING INSTRUCTION 7-A
RECOVERY OF BIOLOGICAL SPECIMENS FROM SUB-ORBITAL AND ORBITAL FLIGHT

REVISED THIRD EDITION

This instruction accompanies CONTINGENCY FILM 7-A and is to be posted at
every recovery bay. The film is to be shown to all incoming staff before
first duty.

The procedures are simple. They have been tested. Follow them in order.
Do not improvise.

    ONE     Confirm the hatch seal is intact before approach.

    TWO     Record the specimen's respiration before you record
            anything else.

    THREE   If the specimen is warm to the touch, withdraw and note
            the time.

    FOUR    Do not weigh the specimen more than once. Discard the
            second figure.

    FIVE    If the specimen is conscious, do not allow it to observe
            your hands.

    SIX     If the specimen is conscious and has already observed your
            hands, do not return to the enclosure. Send someone whose
            hands it has not seen.

    SEVEN   Do not describe your work to the specimen. Do not describe
            your work near the specimen. Do not describe your work.

YOU ARE PERMITTED TO BE AFRAID.
YOU ARE NOT PERMITTED TO BE INTERESTING.

AMENDMENT SLIP, ISSUED WITH THIS EDITION

Step FOUR is withdrawn. Both figures are to be recorded. See
QUARANTINE-PROTOCOL-PROCEDURES.

Steps FIVE and SIX are retained without amendment and their standing is
raised from advisory to mandatory.

The Branch records that steps FIVE, SIX and SEVEN were drafted in
█████████████ against no evidence whatever. They were written by
███████████████, who was asked afterwards what they were for and who
said that he had been unable to think of a reason a specimen should not
watch your hands and had therefore assumed there was one.

They are now the only three steps in this instruction that the events of
23 November did not overtake.
